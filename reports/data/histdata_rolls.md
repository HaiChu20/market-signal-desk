HistData futures-era bars remain stored with their feed, stale and roll metadata, but no adjusted futures prices are used; all `adj_bid_*` columns are NaN. The rows below are retained as historical diagnostics and are not an accepted continuous futures series. Further roll reconstruction is paused pending a homogeneous-source decision (for example CME futures via Databento).
